Workplace: Aligning OPC and Healthcare Stakeholders

2026-07-27 · 20-30 min · Echo English for Lance
Workplace Communication
🎯 Today's Goal 2 min

Practice a clear SA opening for a meeting with an OPC client and a healthcare stakeholder. Your focus is alignment before solution design.

📥 Input 5 min
In a mixed customer meeting, the SA should align business goals, compliance concerns, and technical constraints before recommending a cloud architecture.
在混合客户会议中，SA 要先对齐业务目标、合规顾虑和技术限制，再推荐云架构。
🧩 Key Expressions 8 min
Could we first align on the main business goal?
我们可以先对齐核心业务目标吗？
From a healthcare perspective, what data needs the highest level of protection?
从医疗场景来看，哪些数据需要最高级别保护？
For an OPC business, we should keep the solution lean but scalable.
对于 OPC 业务，我们应该让方案轻量但可扩展。
I suggest we separate must-have requirements from nice-to-have features.
我建议区分必须项和锦上添花项。
Let me capture the open questions and owners.
我来记录待确认问题和负责人。
💬 Mini Dialogue 7 min

Client: We want the system to be secure, affordable, and ready in one month.

Lance: Understood. Could we first align on the main business goal and the compliance requirements?

Client: The healthcare team is most concerned about patient data.

Lance: That makes sense. I suggest we separate must-have security controls from nice-to-have features, then design a phased plan.

🎤 Output Task 8 min

Give a 60-second meeting opening. Include: business goal, healthcare compliance, OPC budget, and next step.

Start with: Thanks everyone. Before we discuss the architecture, I would like to align on...
