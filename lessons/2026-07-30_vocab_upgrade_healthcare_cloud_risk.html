🔥 5 Expression Upgrades 15 min
Basic: data safety
Better: data protection and access control
数据安全 - 数据保护和访问控制
For patient records, data protection and access control should be part of the initial design.
Basic: system connection
Better: system interoperability
系统连接 - 系统互操作性
We need to confirm system interoperability with the hospital information system.
Basic: problem process
Better: incident response process
问题处理流程 - 事件响应流程
A clear incident response process reduces the impact of security events.
Basic: backup plan
Better: business continuity plan
备份计划 - 业务连续性计划
Healthcare services need a business continuity plan because downtime affects patients.
Basic: who does what
Better: roles and responsibilities
谁做什么 - 角色和职责
Before migration, we should define roles and responsibilities between the customer and cloud provider.
