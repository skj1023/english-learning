Speaking: OPC Scope and Tradeoff

2026-07-29 · 20-30 min · Echo English for Lance
Speaking Practice
🎯 Today's Goal 2 min

Practice a realistic conversation where an OPC customer wants enterprise level features with a small budget and a short timeline.

🧩 Speaking Tools 6 min
Given the timeline, I recommend we start with the core workflow.
考虑到时间，我建议先从核心流程开始。
There is a tradeoff between speed, cost, and customization.
速度、成本和定制化之间存在取舍。
We can design this as phase one, then expand later.
我们可以把它设计成第一阶段，之后再扩展。
I would not recommend overbuilding at this stage.
我不建议这个阶段过度建设。
💬 Role Play 10 min

Client: We are a small company, but we want a full AI customer service platform in three weeks.

Lance: I understand the goal. Given the timeline, I recommend we start with the core workflow.

Client: But we also need analytics, multilingual support, and CRM integration.

Lance: Those are valuable features, but there is a tradeoff between speed, cost, and customization. We can design the core platform as phase one, then expand later.

"Why can't we build everything in the first release?"
